Want people to like you more? Ask, then ask again.

In 2017, Harvard researchers (Huang, Yeomans, Brooks, Minson and Gino) found that people who ask more questions are liked more. Follow-up questions did the most work. Speed daters who asked them got more second dates.

Zeno of Citium said it around 300 BC. A young man in Athens wouldn't stop talking, so Zeno told him we've got two ears and one mouth to listen more and talk less. It's in Diogenes Laertius, Lives 7.23.

If you've been searching how to be more likable, here's the cheapest answer zeno stoicism gives you: let them talk, and prove you heard.

I'm trying it today. Follow up twice before you talk about yourself.
