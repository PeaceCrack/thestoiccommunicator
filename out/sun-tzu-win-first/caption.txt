Why does the same person win every meeting they walk into?

Around 500 BC, King Helü of Wu handed Sun Tzu 180 palace women and asked him to drill them. Sun Tzu explained every command before the drum sounded. When they laughed, he enforced it. The next drill was flawless. Sima Qian records it in the Shiji.

The Art of War puts it in one line: the victorious strategist only seeks battle after the victory has been won.

For meeting preparation, that means 3 laws. Make the ask clear before you arrive. Run a pre-mortem, Gary Klein's trick from HBR, the day before. Close your own gaps and let the other side open theirs.

Most people read Sun Tzu Art of War quotes as battle advice. I read them as meeting advice.

Save this before your next big meeting.
